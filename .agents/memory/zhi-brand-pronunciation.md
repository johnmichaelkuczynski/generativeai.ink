---
name: ZHI brand pronunciation
description: Pronunciation and spoken-URL rules for ZHI Systems promotional media.
---

Speak “ZHI Systems” as “Zhee Systems,” never “Zee Aitch Eye Systems.” In
narration, speak web addresses naturally as the domain plus “dot” and the
top-level-domain letters; do not say the protocol or trailing slash.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected letter-by-letter pronunciation and
spoken “https” as robotic and unnatural.

**How to apply:** Use this rule in every voiceover, video, podcast, ad, or
spoken promotional asset for ZHI Systems.
